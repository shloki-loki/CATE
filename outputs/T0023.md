# T0023: UI and HUD (done)

**Pull request:** https://github.com/shloki-loki/CATE/pull/10 (branch `cate/T0023-hud`, based on current `main` ab41453, which includes T0020 vehicles).

## What was built (game/src/ui/hud/, plain DOM/CSS + a 2D-canvas minimap, updated every render frame)
- **Speed displayed:** the speedometer (bottom right, shown only in a car) gives km/h, gear, a rev bar and a damage bar. It is now part of the HUD layer, and its digits use tabular figures so they don't jitter.
- **Mini-map shows position** (`minimap.ts`, bottom left): centred on the player with a player arrow, heading-up, with a north marker on the rim. It zooms out with speed.
  - Roads, buildings, beach and sea are drawn once into an off-screen canvas from the `MinimapMap` layout that `createDemoScene()` now returns. Each frame is one `drawImage` plus the blips.
  - The objective blip sticks to the rim when it's off the map. Entities with a `Blip` component are drawn too.
  - The canvas stays sharp on HiDPI screens and after resizes (ResizeObserver).
- **Mission indicator** (`missionPanel.ts`, top right; it drops below the F3 debug overlay on windows narrower than 1100 px): title, step n/N, timer, objective text and distance to the objective. It flashes when the objective changes, and a centred banner shows the result.
- **Mission source:** `game/missions/CheckpointRun` is a "WARM-UP LAP" with 6 checkpoints on the demo roads and a yellow beacon.
  - The clock starts when the car moves. It keeps the best time and restarts after 5 s.
  - It writes a `MissionStatus` component and emits `missionStarted`, `missionStep` and `missionPassed`.
  - It's a placeholder: the later mission-system task can replace it and keep the same component.
- **Architecture rules kept:** `ui/` never imports `game/`. `src/hudBridge.ts` fills a `HudModel` snapshot each frame, and the boundary test passes.
- `window.__neon.hud` exposes what the HUD shows, for tests and DevTools.

## Checks
- speed displayed: the e2e test checks that the speed text matches the car's real speed while driving (>20 km/h).
- mini-map shows position: the unit tests check the projection (player at the centre, forward always up, not mirrored). The e2e test checks that the minimap centre equals the car position and that the canvas changes as the car moves.
- no UI glitches:
  - The e2e test checks that no HUD element overlaps another or leaves the viewport at 1920x1080, 1366x768, 1280x720, 1024x768, 900x600 and 800x500. It also checks that the canvas resolution matches its CSS size times the DPR, and that there are no console errors or warnings.
  - The HUD layer is `pointer-events: none`, so mouse look still works.
  - Text is only rewritten when it changes.
