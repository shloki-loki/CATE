# T0026: Character controller (done)

**Pull request: https://github.com/shloki-loki/CATE/pull/12** (branch `cate/T0026-character-controller`, targets `main`; the owner reviews and merges)

**It replaces PR #7.** The character controller from T0021 (PR #7) could not merge because it conflicts with the vehicle PR #8. This PR merges that work onto current `main`, resolves the conflicts, and wires the character to the real T0020 vehicles. PR #7 can be closed once #12 is merged.

## What it does (in `game/`)
- **Movement:**
  - A capsule driven by Rapier's `KinematicCharacterController`.
  - WASD moves relative to the camera. Jog 4.6 m/s, Shift to sprint at 7.2 m/s, with smooth acceleration.
  - It steps up curbs and stairs up to 0.45 m and climbs slopes up to 50°.
- **Jumping:** Space jumps 1.15 m. A jump still works for a moment after walking off a ledge, and a press just before landing is remembered. Hitting a ceiling stops the rise.
- **Collision:**
  - Walls, buildings and parked cars block the player, who slides along them.
  - Crates get pushed. The push is capped at 600 N, so a parked car stays put.
- **Camera:**
  - On foot, a spring-arm follow camera with mouse look and zoom. It sweeps a sphere so it never goes inside walls.
  - In a car, T0020's chase camera takes over. Getting out hands the view back with no cut.
- **Game loop:** the player and camera systems are added in `main.ts` in the existing phase order.
- **Vehicles:** the player starts on foot beside the sports car. F gets in and the car drives as before; F gets out on the driver side. Exit spots that are blocked by a building or another car are skipped. The empty car holds its handbrake.

## Fixes found while integrating
- **Car direction mix-up:** T0021 assumed cars face -Z, but T0020 cars face +Z with +X as their left side. The player's facing after getting out and the side they got out on were both mirrored. Fixed.
- **Pushing:** Rapier's built-in pushing shoved a parked 1.4 t car about 3 m when the player walked into it. The new integration test caught this. It is replaced by the force-limited push.
- **Camera snap on getting out:** the spring arm snapped in instantly whenever it got shorter, which made the camera jump 2.7 m when leaving a car. Now only a real obstruction snaps it; otherwise it eases.

## Evidence for each check
- **Character moves on flat ground:**
  - Unit test (headless, real Rapier, same systems and order as `main.ts`): W moves the player more than 7 m in 2 s. It stays on a straight line within 5 cm and at ground height within 2 cm, then returns to `idle`.
  - Browser test (production build, headless Chrome): holding W runs the player forward in a straight line.
- **Collision stops movement against obstacles:**
  - Unit tests: walls block and slide; 5 s of sprinting into a building does not get through; a 1 m wall needs a jump; ceilings stop jumps.
  - Strafing into the parked sports car for 2 s stops at its side, and the car moves less than 0.3 m.
  - Browser test: the same push into the parked car is blocked, and the car is still at its parked spot (within 0.3 m).
- **Camera smoothly follows the character:**
  - Unit tests: the camera stays behind; while running, no frame-to-frame step is larger than 2× the per-tick run distance.
  - Handing the view back after getting out moves the camera at most 0.6 m per frame (measured about 0.16 m); the FOV goes back to 60°.
  - Older T0021 tests cover the short-way turn across ±180° and the snap after a teleport.
  - Browser test: the camera stays behind the running player, and a mouse drag orbits it.
- **Controller integrates without breaking the vehicle module:**
  - All 28 T0020 vehicle unit tests pass unchanged.
  - New integration test: the player gets in with F; the car reaches more than 40 km/h with the player riding along and the chase camera behind it; it brakes to a stop; the player gets out on the driver side; the car then ignores the throttle.
  - The browser car test now presses F first, then drives, steers and brakes. It also checks getting out.

## Limits
- **Cars hitting the player:** a moving car that hits the on-foot player stops as if it hit a post, because the capsule is kinematic. Knockdown and damage are for the combat or pedestrian tasks.
- **No animations:** there is no get-in or get-out animation, and the mannequin is still a placeholder (T0018). No gamepad.
- **Playground location:** the T0021 on-foot playground (stairs, ramps, ledge) is now at about (100, 58), east of downtown, rather than at the spawn.
- **No CI for `game/`:** agents cannot edit `.github/workflows/`; all tests above were run locally.
- **Other open PRs:** I did not check whether PR #9 (procedural city) or PR #10 (HUD) conflict with this branch. Both touch the demo scene and `main.ts`, so whichever merges later may need a rebase.
