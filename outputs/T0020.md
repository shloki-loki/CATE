# T0020: Vehicle physics — done

**Pull request: https://github.com/shloki-loki/CATE/pull/8** (branch `cate/T0020-vehicle-physics`, targets `main`; the owner reviews and merges)

The game is now drivable in the browser. You start in a sports car on the avenue, with four parked cars and the crates at the crossing to drive through or crash into. Controls: W/S throttle and brake (hold S at a standstill to reverse), A/D steer, Space handbrake, R to put the car back on its wheels, mouse drag to look, F3 for the overlay.

**Physics engine:** the brief said "Ammo.js or Cannon.js". The approved architecture (T0016) picked **Rapier**, and the merged skeleton (T0019, PR #6) already runs on it. So I used Rapier's `DynamicRayCastVehicleController`, the same raycast-vehicle model as Ammo.js's `btRaycastVehicle`. Adding a second physics engine to the same world would not work well.

## Evidence for each check
- **Vehicle responds to input:**
  - Headless tests: the car stays still with no input; drives forward on throttle; steers right and left the correct way; brakes from 100 km/h in 30–55 m (measured 38–53 m) and then reverses; R rights an upside-down car.
  - In the real browser build (Playwright, headless Chromium): holding W takes it above 25 km/h, D turns it right, and S stops it. No console errors.
- **Collision detection works:**
  - A wall hit stops the car at the wall face (under 5 cm penetration), sends a `collision` event (other side `null` = static world) with a contact point, and applies damage.
  - At 216 km/h the car does not pass through a 20 cm wall (continuous collision detection is on).
  - Car into a parked car: both cars get the event and damage, and the parked car is shoved.
  - Pushing against a wall sends no events and does no damage. A wrecked car emits `death` and loses drive.
  - In the browser run, the car really hit a parked sedan (health 1000 → 802).
- **Physics stable:**
  - 30 s at rest: under 1 mm of movement per tick.
  - Full-lock cornering at 100 km/h rolls none of the 5 classes over.
  - A ramp jump of about 5 m plus kerbs lands upright.
  - 2 minutes of random driving with 5 cars among walls: no NaN, nothing falls through the ground, no runaway speed.

Test totals:
- `npm run check`: typecheck, lint and build pass; 54/54 unit tests (28 of them new).
- `npm run test:e2e`: 2/2 pass.
- Repo Python tests: 32 OK.

## Limits
- There is still **no CI job for `game/`**: agents cannot edit `.github/workflows/`. Everything above was run locally in this job.
- Not done here:
  - Wheels have no simulated spin of their own, so there is no wheelspin or ABS.
  - Rapier's side-friction stiffness barely changes steady cornering. It is the friction-slip (`grip`) value that sets cornering, so the handbrake slide comes mainly from the locked rear.
  - Damage is health only: no smoke, fire or deformation, and no engine sound.
  - No lil-gui tuning panel, no headlights at night, no on-foot enter/exit (T0021).
- Speeds and grip are tuned against typical real-car figures. I have not checked the feel on a real GPU or with a human driver; SwiftShader ran at about 6–34 fps.
- The car models are placeholder boxes (T0018 will replace them). Paint is non-metallic until there is an environment map.
