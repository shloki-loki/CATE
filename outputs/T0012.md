## What you'll see
**Every page: brightness button at the top right.** Each page now has a top bar: the page title on the left, and the light/dark button and sign-out on the right. On your first visit it follows your computer's setting. Your choice is remembered and applies to all open tabs. If you pick the theme that matches your computer, CATE goes back to following your computer.

**Sidebar.** The bottom bar with the "show archived projects" button is gone, as you asked. Archived projects stay hidden. You can still archive a single project from its top bar.

**Home page:**
- A short status row showing each connection, with dot + "% left" or "not connected", plus a "+ Add" chip.
- A clear "Connect your first model" / "Turn on the instant supervisor" card when one is needed.
- Four starting-point cards: Research, Write, Build, Improve CATE.
- A list of your recent projects.

**Models page.** It's easier to read, and it's now clear how to add more:
1. A **"How to add a model or API" guide** in 4 steps at the top, plus a **"+ Add model or API"** button in the top bar.
2. An **"Add a model or API" section**:
   - Pick Claude subscription, ChatGPT subscription or API key.
   - For an API key, cards for Groq, Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral, Cerebras and DeepSeek, each showing the free allowance, a "Get a key ↗" link and the secret name.
   - Name the connection.
   - Connect instructions with copy buttons.
   - A side panel explains how to add a **new model** to the list CATE chooses from (`config/models.json`, by asking in chat or by editing the file through a pull request).
3. **Connection cards are short now**, two per row: status, limits, "N models on". The model ticks, "which model for which job", budget and reconnect steps are folded under *Configure* / *Reconnect*. A card that isn't connected yet shows "Finish connecting" open, with a "Done: resume" button.
4. A **team map**, which is the node design you asked about: Supervisor → each connection → the models it uses. A moving line means a job is running there.
5. The **"Which model for what" table** has a sticky header and a job filter (All / Planning / Research / …). The filter sorts the table and marks the best model for that job.

**Colours and type.** Calm neutral greys with the teal accent used only for the main actions, flatter cards, smaller corners and numbers that line up. It also fixes the text-contrast failures found in the research, so small grey text is easier to read in both themes.

## Shift+Enter: I followed what you asked for, not the brief
The brief I was given said to *switch off* automatic numbering. You asked for the opposite ("Cannot add bullets while typing", "numbering do not comes in automatically when I do shift + enter after a number"), and so did the research. So now:
- **Shift+Enter after `1. something` starts `2. `.** After `- something` it starts `- `. Checkboxes `- [ ]` continue too.
- Shift+Enter on an empty item ends the list. Shift+Enter on a normal line is just a new line. Enter still sends.
- If you ever want it off, click **"Auto lists: on"** under the chat box. Your browser remembers the setting.
