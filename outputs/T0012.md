## 1. On phones, Send no longer goes off-screen
- **What was wrong:** at 375 px wide, the buttons under the chat box didn't fit on one row. The page became wider than the screen and Send was partly cut off.
- **The fix:** on screens 560 px wide or less (phones):
  - The **Compare** button shows only its icon. Screen readers still read it as "Compare".
  - The buttons have a little less padding and smaller gaps.
  - The "Models: auto" / "2 models" label can shorten itself with "…" before anything overflows.
- Wider screens look the same as before.
- **Checked in headless Chrome** on Home and on chats with 2 or 3 models picked, at 8 widths from 320 px to 1280 px. **24/24 cases passed:**
  - The page was never wider than the screen.
  - Send was fully visible and was the element under a click at its centre.
  - The buttons stayed on one row.
  - At 375 px, Send now ends at x=350. Before, it ended at x=391, past the edge.

## 2. Arrow keys work on the Type and API choices
- Under "Add a model or API", the "What are you adding?" and "Which API?" cards are now **real radio buttons with labels**. They are hidden visually, and the card shows which one is chosen. Before, they were plain buttons that only reacted to clicks.
- The browser now handles the keys:
  - **Tab** goes into a group at the chosen card.
  - The **arrow keys** move the choice.
  - The focused card shows a teal outline.
- Each provider's **"Get a key ↗" link** sits outside its radio, so it is still its own Tab stop. Enter on it opens the key page in a new tab and leaves your choice as it was.
- I replaced the test's blanket ban on `onKeyDown` with checks of the actual behaviour:
  - the choices are native radios with labels and no `role="radio"`;
  - the hidden radios stay focusable;
  - each link is outside its label.
- **Checked in Chrome, 13/13 passed:**
  - With Claude focused, Right picks ChatGPT and Left goes back.
  - Down picks API key, which shows the "Which API?" step.
  - Tab lands on Groq, which has a visible outline, and Down picks Gemini.
  - Tab goes from Groq to Groq's "Get a key" link, and the next Tab goes to Gemini's link.
  - Enter on a link opens a new tab and Groq stays picked.
- I also submitted the form for real, against a copy of the data, with DeepSeek picked. It saved the connection with the right type (`runner: api`) and provider (`preset: deepseek`), so the form still works without the old hidden fields.
