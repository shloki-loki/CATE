# T0021: Character Controller (done)

**Pull request:** https://github.com/shloki-loki/CATE/pull/7 (branch `cate/T0021-character-controller`, opened against `main`, not merged).

## What was built (in `game/`)
- **Physics:** a kinematic capsule driven by Rapier's `KinematicCharacterController`, through new `PhysicsAPI` methods: `createCharacter`, `moveCharacter`, `placeCharacter`, `castSphere` and `setBodyEnabled`. It steps up curbs and stairs (up to 0.45 m), walks up slopes up to 50° and slides down steeper ones, stays snapped to the ground, slides along walls and pushes crates.
- **Player** (`src/game/player/`):
  - Movement is relative to the camera. Speed ramps up and down at a constant rate: jog 4.6 m/s, Shift to sprint at 7.2 m/s. The body turns smoothly toward the direction of movement.
  - Space jumps 1.15 m. A jump still works for a moment after walking off a ledge, and a press just before landing is remembered. Hitting a ceiling stops the rise. Falling out of the world respawns you.
  - A state machine on the `Character` component.
  - F enters or exits the nearest vehicle, or fires an `interact` event for an `Interactable`.
  - A placeholder mannequin with a procedural walk/run cycle.
- **Follow camera** (`src/game/camera/followCamera.ts`):
  - Spring arm behind the player, following the interpolated position with light smoothing.
  - Mouse look (click to capture the mouse, or drag) and wheel zoom.
  - A sphere sweep against buildings and ground pulls the camera in at once when something is in the way, so it never goes inside walls. When the player is in a car it follows the car.
- **Input bug fixed:** a quick tap between two simulation ticks used to be lost (Space did not jump during slow frames).
- **Demo:** the player spawns next to a small test playground (stairs, ramps, steep slope, 1 m ledge, low roof, wall, crates). The demo car can be entered with F; it cannot be driven until T0020.
- The README documents the controls, the API, and the enter/exit contract for T0020 (`Driver`/`occupants`/`vehicleEntered`/`vehicleExited`/`CameraRig.follow`).

## Against the checks
- **Movement smooth:**
  - A unit test checks that speed never changes by more than the acceleration allows in one 60 Hz tick.
  - This test caught a real problem, which I fixed. Pushing the capsule down while grounded made Rapier drop whole ticks of movement, which would show as a hitch. Separately, using the post-collision speed as the next speed stopped the capsule on curbs.
  - Rendering interpolates between ticks.
- **Camera follows:**
  - Unit tests: it sits behind and looks at the player; it follows a sprinting target with no backward motion, no jumps and less than 0.5 m of lag; it turns the short way across ±180°; it snaps after a teleport.
  - Browser test: the camera stays behind the running player, and a mouse drag swings it around the player.
- **Environment collision works:** unit tests with real Rapier cover:
  - walls block and slide; 5 s of sprinting into a 40 m building does not get through;
  - stairs and curbs are climbed, but a 1 m wall is not unless you jump;
  - ceilings stop a jump; gentle ramps can be climbed and steep ones cannot;
  - crates get pushed; vehicle exit picks the free side when one side is against a wall;
  - the camera is never inside a building or under the ground.
