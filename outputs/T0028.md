# T0028: UI and HUD (done)

**Pull request:** https://github.com/shloki-loki/CATE/pull/14 (branch `cate/T0028-hud-health-wanted`, based on `main` 3a9ce3a)

## Starting point
`main` already had part of the HUD from T0023 (PR #10): a speedometer, a mini-map and a mission panel. This job adds the parts that were missing: **health and wanted-level indicators**, and the gameplay that raises the wanted level on collisions. The `game/wanted` module was an empty stub before this.

## What was built
- **Wanted level** (`game/src/game/wanted/wanted.ts`, `WantedSystem`): a `WantedLevel` component holds 0–5 stars.
  - Every collision involving the player's car counts as an offence: +1 star, or +2 for ramming a police car.
  - One crash produces several contact events, so offences are debounced to one per crash (1 s).
  - Stars drop one at a time after a quiet spell (10 s + 3 s per star).
  - It emits a new `wantedChanged` event.
- **Player health** (`game/src/game/shared/playerHealth.ts`, `PlayerHealthSystem`): the player has 100 HP, separate from the car's damage bar.
  - Hard crashes hurt the driver by 0.12 × the car's collision damage.
  - Health regenerates at 2 HP/s after 6 s without damage.
  - Death and respawn are not implemented; they are left for the combat/player tasks.
- **HUD overlay** (`game/src/ui/hud/statusPanel.ts` plus CSS): HTML/CSS on top of the canvas, with `pointer-events: none`. It sits above the mini-map and shows:
  - five wanted stars;
  - a health bar with the number (and an armour bar when armour > 0).

  Values are written to the page only when they change. A new star makes the stars flash, the lit stars blink in the last 4 s before one is lost, and the health bar turns red when low. Windows ≤ 700 px wide or ≤ 540 px tall get a compact layout.
- The speedometer and mini-map from T0023 are unchanged: speed updates every frame, and the mini-map is centred on the player with a heading arrow.
- `hudBridge.ts` fills the new `HudModel.status`, and `ui/` still never imports `game/`. `window.__neon.hud` now also shows `health` and `wanted`.

## Checks
- **Speed updates in real time:** the existing e2e test passes. It checks that the shown speed matches the car's real speed while driving.
- **Mini-map shows player position:** the existing unit and e2e tests pass. They check that the mini-map centre equals the car's position and that the map changes as the car moves.
- **Wanted level increments on collisions:**
  - New unit tests with real physics cover:
    - no stars while driving without hitting anything;
    - +1 star after ramming a parked car, counted once per crash;
    - +2 stars for a police car;
    - repeated wall crashes add one star each, up to a maximum of 5;
    - stars decay one at a time after exactly the quiet time.
  - A new e2e test drives into the parked sedan in the browser. It checks that ≥ 1 star is lit and that the lit stars match the game state.
- **Responsive layout on common browsers:**
  - Chromium: the e2e overlap and viewport test (now including the health/wanted panel) passes at 1920x1080, 1366x768, 1280x720, 1024x768, 900x600 and 800x500.
  - WebKit (Safari engine): the full game ran with no overlaps at those sizes, and a crash lit 1 star.
  - Firefox: headless Firefox on the runner has no WebGL2, so the game shows its "WebGL2 not available" screen. I checked the HUD CSS there by injecting the live HUD markup: no overlaps at any of those sizes. I could not play the game in Firefox.

## Testing done in this job
