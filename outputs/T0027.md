# T0027: Procedural city generation (done)

**Pull request: https://github.com/shloki-loki/CATE/pull/13** (branch `cate/T0027-procedural-city`, targets `main`; the owner reviews and merges)

The game now boots into a city generated at runtime from a seed. This PR **replaces PR #9** (T0022). #9 conflicts with `main` and was written before the HUD and vehicles existed. I rebuilt the city on current `main`, starting from #9's road-graph code and adding the glTF export and T0018 naming this job asks for. PR #9 can be closed.

## What was built (`game/src/game/world/`)
- **`generateCity(options) → CityData`**:
  - Pure and deterministic per seed, with no Three.js and no Rapier.
  - Road grid: 8×8 irregular lines. The outer ring and every third line are 16 m arterials (4 lanes, double yellow line); the rest are 11 m streets (2 lanes, dashed line).
  - Streets that are not needed for connectivity become 5 m alleys with raised walkways. The `RoadGraph` is always connected.
  - **Ground tiling:** junctions, roads, alleys, walkways, blocks, open land and an east beach are rectangles that tile the land exactly. A sea surrounds the land.
  - Blocks are raised 0.15 m with kerbs and have a 3 m sidewalk ring.
  - **Buildings:** lots get buildings by district (downtown, beach, residential, industrial) in the T0018 §2.1 styles:
    - `downtown_tower`, `downtown_office`
    - `beach_hotel`, `beach_shop`
    - `res_house`, `res_lowrise`
    - `ind_warehouse`
    
    Each has floors (T0018 floor heights), podium and setback tiers, and a flat, hip or gable roof.
  - Also holds spawn points, zones and a warm-up lap on the arterials.
  - The default seed 1986 gives 326 buildings, 106 roads and 7 chunks of 256 m.
- **Naming (T0018 §8):**
  - chunks `c_<x>_<z>`
  - buildings `b_<chunkX>_<chunkZ>_<index>`, numbered from 0 per chunk
  - materials `mat_asphalt|sidewalk|kerb|grass|sand|water|roof|facade_<style>`
  - plain lowercase mesh parts
  
  The regular expressions are in `CITY_NAMES`.
- **`cityMesher.ts`:**
  - **Runtime:** one merged mesh per material per chunk, with a `buildingId → index range` table. That is about 45 meshes and 90k triangles for the default city.
  - **No overlaps:** lane markings and window rows are cut into the surfaces, not laid on top of them.
  - **Export mode:** one mesh node per building.
- **`cityColliders.ts`:** a ground slab, 0.15 m kerb boxes, one body per building with a box per tier, and invisible shore walls.
- **glTF export:**
  - `exportCityGlb(city)` uses `src/assets/gltfExport.ts`: Three.js GLTFExporter with the Node `FileReader` shim from T0018 §9.
  - `npm run city:export -- --seed N --out file.glb` writes the file and runs the Khronos glTF validator. It exits with an error on any validation error.
  - `gltf-validator` is a new dev dependency, at the version T0018 pinned.
- **`main.ts`:**
  - loads the city;
  - spawns the player car and parked cars from `city.spawnPoints`;
  - runs the warm-up lap on the city's arterials;
  - feeds the HUD minimap from `cityMinimap(city)`;
  - reports `window.__neon.city` (seed, counts, `loadMs`, `readyAtMs`).

## Evidence for each check
All runs were local in this job.
- **City loads within 5 seconds:**
  - Node unit test: generate + meshes + colliders took **94 ms**.
  - Chromium e2e test (production build, SwiftShader): `loadMs` **207 ms**, and the city was in the scene **1,113 ms** after navigation. Both are asserted to be under 5,000 ms.
- **Streets are navigable by vehicle and character:**
  - **Lap drive:** a real Rapier sports car (T0020 physics) with a simple autopilot drives the **whole warm-up lap** for seeds 1986 and 2024. Each took about 97 s simulated, top speed 53 km/h, **0 collisions**, min up.y 0.999. The mission's `CheckpointRun` reports the lap passed from the car's real positions.
  - **Side streets:** a sedan drives **20 side-street runs** (10 streets, both directions) with 0 collisions.
  - **Control test:** driving into a building does register collisions, so "0 collisions" is meaningful.
  - **Character controller:** a Rapier kinematic character controller with T0026's settings (capsule r 0.35 m, half height 0.55 m, step 0.45 m):
    - steps up a kerb from the road (feet at ≥ 0.10 m);
    - walks the sidewalk ring;
    - steps back down;
    - is stopped by the walls of 15 buildings.
  - **Flood fill:** a 0.5 m flood fill (3 seeds) shows every road, alley, junction and sidewalk sample is reachable on foot from the spawn point.
  - **Graph:** the road graph is connected for 8 seeds × 4 drop rates (including 100%), and every block borders a drivable road.
- **Building meshes follow the naming convention from T0018:**
  - For 8 seeds, ids match `b_<cx>_<cz>_<i>`, are unique, are numbered 0..n per chunk, and sit in the chunk of their footprint centre.
  - Runtime chunk groups, part names and material names match the T0018 patterns.
  - The exported `.glb` gets **0 errors, 0 warnings, 0 infos** from the Khronos glTF validator 2.0.0-dev.3.10.
  - Loaded back with Three.js `GLTFLoader`, its building node names equal the city's building ids, the building nodes sit under `c_*` nodes, and every material name matches `mat_*`.
- **No overlapping geometry:**
  - Ground surfaces are pairwise disjoint and their total area equals the land area exactly (8 seeds); the sea does not overlap the land.
  - Buildings are inside their lots, keep the sidewalk ring clear, are at least 1.5 m apart, and never sit on roads, alleys or walkways.
  - Tiers stack inside each other.
  - Mesh face-area sums show each ground surface, kerb face, building wall and roof is drawn **exactly once**. No markings sit on top of the road, and no building bottoms or hidden floors are drawn.

## Limits
- **Layout:** roads are axis-aligned; there are no curves, bridges or islands. Footprints are rectangles; there are no L or U shapes yet.
- **Not streamed:** there is no ChunkStreamer, so the whole city (about 0.6 km across) loads at once. The sun's shadow box still covers only ±120 m around the origin; that is render code I did not change.
- **Looks:** windows are vertex colours, with no textures and no night glow. There is no street furniture (palms, lamps).
- **Export size:** the export is not meshopt-compressed, because T0018's `optimize.ts` step does not exist yet. The default city `.glb` is about 7.5 MB, and it is a tool output, not committed.
- **Frame rate:** not measured on a real GPU. In SwiftShader the debug overlay showed about 35 fps, 195 draw calls and 154k triangles (shadow pass included) at boot.
- **Merge conflicts:**
  - With PR #11: `README.md` and `game/index.ts`.
  - With PR #12: also `main.ts` (#12 already conflicts with `main`).
  
  When #12 is combined with this PR, its on-foot player spawn should use `city.spawnPoints`.
- **CI:** there is no CI job for `game/`, because agents cannot edit `.github/workflows/`.
