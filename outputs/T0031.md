## What the review found and what I changed

### 1. The lap clock started while the car was parked (the failed check)
**Cause:** the reviewer was right.
- `CheckpointRunSystem` gave the lap the on-foot character's position before entry, then the car's centre after it.
- The character starts 2.2 m beside and 0.5 m behind the car, so that switch alone passed the 2 m start threshold.
- Pressing only F therefore started the clock.

**Fix:**
- `game/src/game/missions/systems.ts`: the lap now gets a position only while the player drives (`findPlayer(world)?.inVehicle`). On foot it gets `null`.
- `game/src/game/missions/checkpointRun.ts`:
  - **Before the clock starts:** being on foot clears the start point. It is taken again at the car's position when the player gets in, so the clock starts only once the driven car moves more than 2 m from there. This also works if the player gets out and walks to a different car.
  - **The 'started' event** is sent once per lap, not on every entry.
  - **New on-foot objective:** "Get in a car (F) to start the lap". It bumps the revision so the HUD updates. In the car it still reads "Drive to the yellow checkpoint to start the lap".
  - **Once the lap is running:** getting out does not pause the clock, and you cannot reach checkpoints on foot. It is a driving lap.

**New tests:**
- **Unit** (`tests/unit/hud.test.ts`), pure lap logic:
  - on foot does not count;
  - getting in 2.2 m away and idling, or rolling about 1 m, keeps the clock at 0;
  - getting out and into another car 30 m away takes a new start point, with no second 'started' event;
  - driving away starts the clock;
  - getting out afterwards keeps the clock running but reaches no checkpoint.
- **ECS integration** (`tests/unit/playerVehicle.test.ts`). It uses Rapier, main.ts's exact layout (character 2.2 m beside and 0.5 m behind the driver door) and the same system order:
  - 2 s waiting on foot, then F, then 3 s idling in the car: `MissionStatus.elapsed` stays exactly 0;
  - driving forward: the clock passes 1 s, and the car reaches checkpoint 1.
  - **I checked that this test catches the bug:** with the old system code it fails with "expected 3.099… to be 0".
- **Browser e2e** (`tests/e2e/hud.spec.ts`, first HUD test). This is the reviewer's reproduction:
  - after the player settles plus 120 ticks, the timer shows `0:00.0` and the objective says "Get in a car";
  - after F and 180 ticks (3 s of game time) idling at a speed under 1 km/h, it is still `0:00.0` and says "start the lap";
  - after driving away it is no longer `0:00.0`.

### 2. The HUD viewport test had lost the speedometer
`HUD elements never overlap or leave the screen at common window sizes, on foot and driving` now runs all six sizes twice, from 1920×1080 down to 800×500:
- **On foot:** the speedometer is asserted hidden.
- **While driving:** it gets in, speeds up past 25 km/h so the speedometer shows two digits, then goes through every size again while the car rolls up the empty arterial. At each size the speedometer is asserted visible and included in the overlap and viewport check, together with the minimap backing-store check.
