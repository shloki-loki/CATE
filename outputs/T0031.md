## The game now
Neon Coast boots into the procedural city. You start **on foot** beside the driver door of a parked sports car, facing the first warm-up-lap checkpoint. You can walk, sprint, jump and step up kerbs. **F** gets into the car; drive the city and the warm-up lap; **F** gets out again. The HUD follows you on foot and in the car: minimap, mission panel, health and wanted stars, and the speedometer while driving. Everything already on main still works and is still tested: the skeleton, T0020 vehicle physics, the T0023 HUD and T0028 health/wanted.

## What I kept from which PR, and why

It also has navigation tests: a Rapier car drives the lap with 0 collisions, and a Rapier character climbs kerbs and reaches every sidewalk. #9 predates the HUD and vehicles.

## Integration changes I made (beyond resolving conflicts)
- **`main.ts` start:**
  - all cars are spawned from `city.spawnPoints`, with no driver;
  - the player is created on foot beside the sports car's driver door (`besideDriverDoor`);
  - systems run in this order: city lap → wanted → player health → follow camera → chase camera → player control.
- **`hudBridge.ts`:** T0028's `findPlayer` already uses `PlayerTag`, which the character has. The fix: on foot the minimap heading uses the character's forward axis (−Z). The car's is +Z, so the map would otherwise point backwards when walking.
- **`style.css`:** #12 made the F3 overlay five lines tall, and the e2e test caught it overlapping the mission panel at 900×600. On windows ≤ 1100 px wide the panel now sits at 122 px (it was at 84 px).
- **`window.__neon.city.playerCar`:** added for tests.
- **e2e tests adapted to the on-foot city start:**
  - the HUD tests press F first and also check the on-foot HUD;
  - the crash/wanted test builds up speed and steers into the buildings beside the arterial;
  - the parked-car test reads the car's spot from the city.
- **README:** controls, layout and sections from all PRs merged.
- **Old demo scene:** it and its on-foot playground are no longer booted. They are kept because their unit tests still use them.

## Notes and limits

- **Known gaps carried over from the PRs:**
  - no traffic or pedestrians;
  - a moving car that hits the on-foot player stops as if it hit a post (the capsule is kinematic);
  - no get-in or get-out animation;
  - the city is not streamed (it loads whole in about 0.2 s).
