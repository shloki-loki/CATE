# T0025: Implement vehicle physics (`Vehicle` class) — done

**Pull request: https://github.com/shloki-loki/CATE/pull/11** (branch `cate/T0025-vehicle-class`, targets `main`; the owner reviews and merges)

## Context
Rapier vehicle physics was already merged in T0020 (PR #8): a raycast vehicle with wheel rays, suspension, steering, acceleration and braking. It was only usable through the ECS systems. I did not write a second physics model. This job adds what the brief asked for on top of it: an exported **`Vehicle` class usable from the game loop**, and a **test arena**.

## What changed (`game/`)
- **`src/game/vehicles/Vehicle.ts`, the `Vehicle` class.** One object holds:
  - the Rapier raycast vehicle: chassis rigid body, four wheel rays with spring/damper suspension and tyre friction;
  - the drive controller: engine torque curve, automatic gearbox, brakes, reverse on held brake, handbrake, speed-sensitive Ackermann steering;
  - air drag and rolling resistance;
  - the Three.js model, whose wheels steer, spin and follow the suspension.

  How it plugs into the game loop: `setInput(...)`, then `update(dt)` before `physics.step(dt)`, `postStep()` after it, and `syncView(alpha)` each render frame. Also: `addTo(scene)`, `reset()`, `damage()`, `dispose()`, `view: false` for headless use, and getters (`speed`, `speedKmh`, `gear`, `rpm`, `heading`, `position`, `quaternion`, `forward()`, `up()`).
- **`driveTick.ts`.** The per-tick drive and wheel-pose code moved out of the ECS systems without changes, so the systems and the class share one implementation. A test checks that a class car and an ECS car on the same inputs stay within 1 mm of each other over 12 s.
- **Test arena.** `arena.ts` holds the layout, colliders and scripted drive, `arenaView.ts` the meshes, and `arena.html` plus `src/arena.ts` the page.
  - The arena is a walled 240 m square with a ramp and kerbs.
  - The page drives the class from a plain `GameLoop`, with no ECS.
  - Modes: keyboard driving, `?autodrive` (the scripted 30-second drive) and `?car=compact|sedan|sports|van|police`.
  - `vite.config.ts` now builds both pages.
- **Naming.** `Vehicle` from `./game` stays the ECS component, so existing code is unchanged. The class is `Vehicle` from `./game/vehicles` and `DrivableVehicle` from `./game`.
- The README has a new section with a usage example.

## Evidence for each check
All runs were local in this job.
- **Vehicle can be instantiated and added to a scene:**
  - Unit test, all 5 classes: the model is a child of a Three.js `Scene`, has wheel nodes `wheel_FL/FR/RL/RR`, and starts exactly at the physics body.
  - `dispose()` removes both the body and the model, and is safe to call twice.
  - The browser arena renders the car (screenshot checked by eye).
- **Forward, reverse and steering in a test arena:**
  - Throttle: more than 36 km/h after 3 s, moving along +Z.
  - Right steer lowers the heading by more than 0.4 rad in 1.5 s; left steer raises it.
  - Holding brake stops the car, then it reverses (gear −1, moving backwards at more than 3 m/s).
  - Throttle then drives it forward again.
  - In the browser (Playwright, `arena.html?car=compact`): W, D and S drive, steer and reverse the car.
- **Physics bodies synchronized with the Three.js meshes:**
  - Checked on every tick of a 7 s drive: at `alpha = 1` the mesh position equals the body position (error < 1e-9 m) and rotation agrees within 1e-6 (slerp rounding).
  - At `alpha = 0.5` the mesh is exactly halfway between the last two ticks.
  - Wheel steer and spin equal the physics wheel state, and wheel height follows suspension length.
  - The world position agrees with the body.
  - Inside `GameLoop` at uneven frame times (12–144 fps plus 0.2 s hitches), the drawn car always lay between the last two physics positions.
  - In the browser drive, the largest sync error was 2.4e-15 m.
- **No runtime errors during a 30-second drive:**
  - Headless, all 5 classes ran the full 30 s script: no exceptions or NaN, always upright (up.y > 0.9), stayed inside the walls, did not sink or launch, and were stopped at the end.
  - Browser, sedan with `?autodrive`: 1804 ticks (30.07 s simulated) and no console errors, warnings or failed requests.
  - Measured: max 54.8 km/h, min −31.2 km/h (reverse), about 124° of right turn and 377° of left turn, min up.y 0.999, 0.2 km/h at the end.

## Limits
- The handling is unchanged from T0020 (same defs and drive model). Wheels still have no simulated spin of their own, so there is no wheelspin or ABS.
- The class has `damage()` but does not read contacts itself. In the main game, collision damage still comes from the ECS `VehicleCollisionSystem`.
- Holding brake at a standstill selects reverse (T0020 design), so the scripted drive's final stop uses the handbrake.
- There is still no CI job for `game/`: agents cannot edit `.github/workflows/`.
- Like open PRs #7, #9 and #10, this PR edits `game/src/game/index.ts` and `game/README.md`. Whichever merges later may need a small conflict fix.
- Feel has not been checked on a real GPU or with a human driver. SwiftShader ran the arena at about 14 fps.
