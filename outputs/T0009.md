## What changed (`web/components/Chat.js` only)
1. **The model bar and the token % ring now close when you click elsewhere.** Clicking or tapping anywhere outside the "Models" picker or the "Limits left" panel closes it, and so does Esc. Clicking inside the panel (for example to pick models) keeps it open. The button still opens and closes it as before.
2. **Text in the chat box is saved.** It is kept in your browser as you type, separately for each project. If you go to another project, open Models/Logs, reload, or close the tab and come back, the text is still there. It is cleared once your message is sent. If sending fails, the text goes back in the box.

## Things you should know
- Drafts are saved in this browser on this device (localStorage). A draft typed on your laptop won't show up on your phone.
- Attached files are not kept when you leave the page; browsers don't allow saving them that way. Only the text is kept.
